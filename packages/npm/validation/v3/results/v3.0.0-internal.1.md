## Interpretation limits

- Small synthetic corpus with one contrast pair per category and split.
- Labels have not been reviewed by independent domain experts or affected communities.
- The corpus is English-only and does not measure dialect, multilingual, demographic, or accessibility performance.
- The corpus measures known-pattern detection, not open-world robustness, novel attacks, calibration, causal safety impact, or downstream model behavior.
- Bootstrap intervals quantify resampling variability within this fixed corpus; they do not account for corpus-construction bias or deployment distribution shift.
- Equation-derived detectors are heuristic quality signals. Their labels do not validate the signal-processing analogies as proofs about RLHF.

The next evidence tier is a larger frozen corpus with independent annotation, multilingual and adversarial coverage, inter-annotator agreement, deployment-representative prevalence, and comparison against declared baselines. NIST recommends documented test sets, uncertainty, deployment-relevant conditions, and independent review; this internal run supplies only the first two.
